Why “Daddy”?

The name comes from a simple idea: the best way to learn about money is the way a father explains it to his child, patiently, honestly and without hype.

Finance with Daddy publishes free calculators and beginner-friendly lessons so that anyone can understand saving, investing and taxes before making decisions of their own.
